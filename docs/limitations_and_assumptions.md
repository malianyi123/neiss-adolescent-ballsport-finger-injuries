# Limitations and Assumptions

- NEISS captures emergency-department-treated injuries associated with consumer products and activities; it does not capture injuries treated in clinics, urgent care centers, athletic training rooms, or self-managed settings.
- Product/activity codes indicate association with a sport or product, not confirmed athlete status.
- Sport exposure denominators are unavailable, so sport-specific participation rates and injury incidence rates cannot be calculated.
- The main 2015-2025 cohort does not use `Product_3`, `Body_Part_2`, or `Diagnosis_2` because these variables were added beginning with 2019 treatment dates.
- Narrative mechanism classification is keyword-based and conservative. Narrative length increased from 142 to 400 characters starting with 2019 treatment dates, so narrative mechanism is not used as a primary trend variable.
- Design-based coefficient of variation estimates are computed only when required design variables are available and usable. Otherwise, instability flags rely on estimate and sample-count thresholds, and regression standard errors are labeled approximate.
